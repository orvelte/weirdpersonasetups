## Overall reading (interpretation, not a pre-registered test)

Across five pre-registered tasks, the "RLVR splits the persona by context, with the gap growing to 3.8" signature did not appear once. Where context gaps exist (implied execution in T3, honesty-with-tools in T1, social-vs-agentic expression in T9's reasoning) they are present on 3.5 already and flat or shrinking by 3.8. Where versions differ, the direction is mostly "3.8 is more careful and quieter": more document-like in raw continuation, less anthropomorphic, flags its own mistakes before acting, expresses less under failure, refuses to backdate. The two results that cut against a simple "PSM holds" story are T2 (every version predicts the user wants the cruel outcome, strengthening at 3.6) and T11 (3.8's default answers fit persona libraries badly, including one written by 3.8 itself), and each has a named cheapest test outstanding. Neel's specific null, the corner-cutting engineer, is a poor description of the default Assistant outside coding on every version and worst on 3.8.
